# Topic pack — Health （火柴人健康線）

The second topic line in this workflow, added 2026-11-24. Same pipeline as the
money/discipline duels (`SKILL.md`, Route B), different arena: **the inside of
the body**. Instead of two stick figures racing on a track, two versions of the
same body run side by side — and the counters count what is happening inside:
sugar points in the stomach, melatonin at the brain's door, blood flow in the
legs.

Why health: everyday-habit A/B questions ("lie down after eating, or walk?")
are the same identity-duel structure as "saver vs spender", with an even wider
audience — everyone eats, sleeps and sits. The payoff is shown as a body event,
never written as medical advice.

## Format adaptations (vs the duel format)

- **Interior scene**: the A/B contrast happens inside one body part per video
  (stomach / brain door / leg vein). Both sides are *in motion* in frame 1 —
  one side jams up, the other flows.
- **Bands, not overlays on the scene**: keyframes are composed landscape on a
  solid deep-blue field, then padded with same-colour bands to 9:16 — hook text
  on the top band, counters on the bottom band, scene untouched in the middle.
- **Counter = a body quantity**: sugar points, melatonin count, blood-flow %.
  The losing side's number visibly *piles up or stays at zero*; the winning
  side's number *drains or fills*. Same acceleration logic as the duel format.
- **Closing line is a tiny action, not a lecture**: "walk 10 minutes", "phone
  down 30 minutes before bed", "stand up for 2 minutes every hour". One
  identity question in the caption: which side are you?

## The topics

### H1 — Lying down after a meal vs a 10-minute walk （飯後躺下 vs 飯後散步）

- Hook: 飯後躺下 vs 散步 10 分鐘，你的胃差很多
  ("Your stomach can tell the difference")
- Left (lie down): sugar points pile up in the stomach, 12 → 48, food lump
  asleep and not moving.
- Right (walk): the food lump moves down steadily, sugar points carried away,
  12 → 15.
- Closing line: 吃飽別躺，走 10 分鐘就好。
- Caption angle: which are you after a meal — the couch or the walk?

### H2 — Scrolling in bed vs putting the phone down （睡前滑手機 vs 放下手機）

- Hook: 睡前滑手機，你的睡眠開關被擋在門外
  ("Your sleep switch is stuck outside the door")
- Left (scrolling): blue light keeps the melatonin "moon people" outside the
  door — 0 get in, they hop and panic.
- Right (phone down): moon people file into the head one by one, 1 → 8, the
  figure sleeps deeply.
- Closing line: 睡不著，先放下手機 30 分鐘。
- Caption angle: is your last act before sleep a screen? Try 30 minutes
  without it tonight.

### H3 — Sitting for hours vs standing up every hour （久坐 vs 每小時起身）

- Hook: 久坐 1 小時，你腿裡的血正在塞車
  ("After one hour of sitting, your leg blood is in a traffic jam")
- Left (sitting): red-blood-cell people jammed in the vein, crying faces,
  blood flow at 10%.
- Right (moving): the same cells march through smiling, blood flow at 100%.
- Closing line: 坐一小時，起來走 2 分鐘。
- Caption angle: how many hours a day do you sit? Highest number gets a
  stand-up reminder.

  ### H4 — Drinking water through the day vs forgetting it （下午 3 點喝水 vs 沒喝水）
- Hook: 下午 3 點，你是清醒的，還是頭昏的？
    ("3pm — are you clear-headed, or foggy?")
- Left (forgot water): head foggy by 3pm, the figure slumps at the desk, water bottle untouched — 0 sips all day.
- Right (drank water): same 3pm, same desk — the figure is clear-headed, water bottle half empty, steady sips through the day.
- Closing line: 下午 3 點頭昏？先喝一杯水。
- Caption angle: what time of day does your head go foggy? 3pm is ours.
